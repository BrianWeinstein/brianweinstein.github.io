Harmonographs

A harmonograph is a mechanical device consisting of two or more pendulums attached to a pen. The swinging pendulums control the motion of the pen, tracing out a geometric pattern on a sheet of paper. Since the system is damped by friction, the pattern spirals in on itself as time progresses.
